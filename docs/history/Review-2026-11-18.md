# Review 2026-11-18: twelve topics

Twelve reviewers read mfm on main at 4d92ee1, one topic each: memory, performance and
hot path, robustness, data safety of file operations, Wayland, tests, hacks and code
quality, processes and file descriptors, text and input, configuration, documentation
against code, and build and undefined behaviour. Points settled in docs/history or
CAVEATS.md were left out. The build reviewer ran the suite under ASan and UBSan with no
report.

## Status

All points settled on fix/review-2026-11-18. A test that fails without its fix was
checked by taking each fix out in turn and running the suite. How each point was
settled is at the end. The suite went from 446 to 579 checks.

## Medium

1. **The FAT check from 2026-11-17 does not work** (mfm.c:2273; three reviewers). It
   takes a temporary folder that does not show 0700 for FAT. A FAT stick mounted with
   `dmask=077` or `umask=077`, as automounters often do, shows 0700, so `cp -a` runs
   and fails again on every mode it cannot set. A umask such as 0277 shows 0500 on a
   POSIX file system, which is then taken for FAT and copied without modes. Fix: a
   mode change that must stick (`chmod` 0755, `stat`, back to 0700), or `statfs`
   `f_type`.
2. **fds 0 to 2 are not secured** (main, mfm.c:2371, 2734). Started with stdin, stdout
   or stderr closed, the Wayland socket lands on one of them. `close_range(4, ...)`
   leaves it to the worker and the clipboard helper, so the window outlives mfm, and
   output of cp, rm or MFM_SEND, or mfm's own messages, goes into the socket. Fix: open
   `/dev/null` at the start of `main` until it returns 3 or more.
3. **Ctrl+1/2/3 cannot be reached on AZERTY** (mfm.c:4571). `shortcut_sym` returns
   keysyms below 0x100 as they are, so Ctrl+1 arrives as Ctrl+`&`. Fix: for Ctrl and Alt
   also look at level 1 of the key and take a digit or Latin letter from there.
4. **Ctrl+T ignores `Mwm*terminal`** (mfm.c:1446). mwc goes `MWC_TERMINAL`, then
   `Mwm*terminal`; mfm skips the second and starts its own choice. Fix: read it from
   mwmrc after `MWC_TERMINAL`; mfm.1.
5. **mwc's terminal value is a shell command, mfm splits it into words** (mfm.c:1446).
   `TERM=xterm-256color foot`, `foot -D ~` or `$HOME/bin/term` work in mwc and fail in
   mfm. Fix: run the values from mwc through `/bin/sh -c`, as mwc does; keep
   `split_cmd` for `MFM_*`.
6. **mfm.1 says PgUp and PgDn repeat in the list** (mfm.1:137). In `key_repeats` only
   Up and Down repeat there, PgUp, PgDn, Left and Right only in input fields.

## Low: code

7. **The clipboard helper keeps mfm's stdout and stderr** (mfm.c:2730). A reader of
   mfm's output, `mfm 2>&1 | logger` or a launcher, gets no EOF while the clipboard
   holds. Fix: fds 0 to 2 to `/dev/null` in `cb_child`.
8. **`close_range` is not checked** (mfm.c:2371, 2734). Before Linux 5.9 it fails and
   the child keeps the Wayland socket. Fix: close by hand on failure; the minimum
   kernel and glibc in the README.
9. **SIGKILL can hit a reused PID** (mfm.c:2759). A helper that died without answering
   is reaped at once under `SIG_IGN`. Fix: `pidfd_open` after `fork`,
   `pidfd_send_signal`.
10. **The helper cannot connect after `WAYLAND_SOCKET`** (mfm.c:2659). libwayland
    removes it when mfm connects; without `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` Ctrl+Shift+C fails. Fix:
    CAVEATS.
11. **Trashing a btrfs subvolume root leaves empty trash folders** (mfm.c:1888ff,
    2025). `topdir_trash` stops at the parent, which is on another device, makes
    `.Trash-$uid` there, and the rename fails with EXDEV. Fix: refuse a subvolume root
    as a mount point is refused, or EXDEV in `topdir_trash` when the parent is not on
    the mount.
12. **The buffer cap overflows on 32-bit** (mfm.c:340; two reviewers). `long` is 32
    bits on i386 and armhf. Fix: `int64_t`.
13. **A freed pointer is compared** (mfm.c:353). `buf_free(b)` comes before
    `s->shown == b`. Fix: clear `shown` first.
14. **`tool()` drops arguments past six without a word** (mfm.c:2187; two reviewers).
    Fix: refuse a list that is too long, or size `argv` from it.
15. **Invisible characters left in names** (mfm.c:530): U+00AD, U+034F, the Hangul
    fillers, U+180E, U+2060 to U+2064, tag characters. `a­b.pdf` looks like
    `ab.pdf`. Fix: `G_UNICODE_FORMAT` and the fillers.
16. **Cursor and cut go by code point, not grapheme** (line_key, mid_char at
    mfm.c:3543). Backspace takes half a flag, a cut can fall between e and its accent.
    Fix: Pango's `is_cursor_position`.
17. **`u8_fit` takes Latin-1 bytes 0x80 to 0xbf for continuation bytes** (mfm.c:1542).
    A long Latin-1 name with such a run at the cut can lose its whole stem in
    `free_name` and `trash_into`.
18. **Bad colours and booleans in mfmrc and the environment are ignored silently**
    (mfm.c:793ff). mwc warns, and mfm does for `Mfm*sort` and fonts.
19. **A relative `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` is taken** (mfm.c:711). The XDG spec says to ignore
    it; `home_trash` already does for `XDG_DATA_HOME`. mwc does the same, so both or
    neither.
20. **The damage diff reads both buffers** (mfm.c:397). At 3840x2160 and scale 2 a
    frame that changes little reads up to 66 MB, a few milliseconds. Fix: compare the
    bands paint() can change, or a hash per row.
21. **One damage band from the first to the last changed row** (mfm.c:409). A selection
    near the top and the status line at the bottom damage nearly everything. Fix: one
    rectangle per run, a few at most.
22. **`strxfrm` runs twice per name** (mfm.c:1043), and the keys stay as long as the
    folder is shown, three to five times the names in UTF-8 locales. Fix: one call
    into a large enough buffer; free the keys after the sort.
23. **Type-ahead still normalizes non-ASCII names per key** (mfm.c:607). 100k Cyrillic
    names and no match is 100k allocations per key. Fix: the NFC form next to the key.
24. **A buffer cut to 32767 pixels breaks the maximized size rule** (mfm.c:343). Not
    realistic; a comment.
25. **Duplicated geometry and strings:** the row layout in `show_date` and the loop
    (mfm.c:3762ff), "Hide hidden files" in `menu_label` and `menu_open`, the size
    template beside `human()`. `textw()` is only another name for `shape()`.
26. **Stale comment on `wsurf_buffer`** (mfm.c:330): the scale can be lowered now.
27. **`job_poll` parses JOB_KEEP with three flags** (mfm.c:2489). Clean: collect a line,
    branch on its first byte.
28. **`-Wshadow` hits** (`top`, `cb`, `sel`, `lw`): none a bug yet.

## How they were settled

- **1.** `keeps_modes()`: 0755 and then 0700 must both work and show. Test: a copy
  under umask 0277 keeps its mode 640. FAT itself cannot be mounted in the test box.
- **2.** `main()` fills fds 0 to 2 with `/dev/null` first. Test: mfm with all three
  closed, quit during a hanging copy, the window goes.
- **3.** With Ctrl or Alt a digit on the key's Shift level counts. No test: vinput
  compiles a `us` keymap only.
- **4, 5.** `MWC_TERMINAL`, then `Mwm*terminal`, run with `sh -c`. Tests for both.
- **6, 29 to 33, 35.** mfm.1 and README as the points say; the minimum versions are in
  the README's build section.
- **7.** `/dev/null` on 0 to 2 in `cb_child`. Test: a reader of mfm's output ends when
  mfm quits with the helper still up.
- **8.** `close_from()`: a loop when close_range fails. No test: it needs an old kernel.
- **9.** The helper is killed through a pidfd. No test: it needs a reused pid.
- **10.** CAVEATS.
- **11.** A folder on another device than its parent is not trashed. No test: no btrfs
  in the test box. CAVEATS updated.
- **12, 13.** `int64_t`; `shown` cleared before the free. No test: 32-bit and UB only.
- **14.** `tool()` refuses a list that is too long. No caller has one.
- **15.** Format characters and the Hangul fillers are invisible. Test: a soft hyphen
  shows, by the ink in its row.
- **16.** Cursor and cut by Pango's cursor positions. Test: Backspace takes a flag.
- **17.** `u8_fit()` walks from the front. Test: a name of 255 bytes 0xb0 keeps 248 of
  them in its copy.
- **18.** Words on stderr. Tests for a colour and a boolean.
- **19.** Left as it is: mfm reads mwmrc as mwc does, and mwc takes a relative
  `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` too. A change belongs in both.
- **20.** Left as it is: the diff runs at memory speed, a few milliseconds only at
  3840x2160 and scale 2; bands from paint() or row hashes trade that for damage that
  can be missed.
- **21.** Up to four rectangles, one per run of changed rows. Test from the protocol
  log.
- **22, 23.** One `strxfrm` per name, keys freed after the sort; the composed name kept
  in the entry. No test: no visible change.
- **24, 26.** Comments.
- **25, 27, 28.** Constants for the columns and labels, `textw` gone, `job_poll` by
  line, the shadowing names renamed.
- **34.** `.TH` 2026-11-18.
- **36.** Test: Down held moves several rows.
- **37.** The date test names DejaVu and first checks the name is in the shot.
- **38.** clipget exits 3 for an empty clipboard, and the test wants 3.
- **39.** Worker and helper are named `mfm-job` and `mfm-clip`; `job_settled` leaves out
  `mfm-clip`.
- **40.** `trashed_from` finds an item by the path in its `.trashinfo`.
- **41.** `err_pixels` reads the status line only; the comment names the delete
  question.
- **42.** Tests for each, except the back button (vinput has none) and marks kept when
  Ctrl+Shift+C fails (nothing in the test box makes it fail).
- **43.** files.sh fails without user namespaces.
